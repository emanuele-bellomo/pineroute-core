# Backtest Metrics

This document explains the key performance metrics used in the UniStrat backtest scripts.

### 1. Max DrawDown %
**Max DrawDown (MDD)** is the maximum observed loss from a peak to a trough of a portfolio, before a new peak is attained. It is expressed as a percentage of the peak value.
- **Formula:** `(Peak Value - Trough Value) / Peak Value`
- **Significance:** It measures the "worst-case scenario" for a strategy, indicating the maximum decline an investor would have experienced.

### 2. Profitable %
Also known as the **Win Rate**, this is the percentage of total trades that resulted in a profit.
- **Formula:** `(Number of Winning Trades / Total Number of Trades) * 100`
- **Significance:** It helps understand how often the strategy is correct, though it must be considered alongside the average win/loss size.

### 3. Number of Trades
The total count of completed trades (buy followed by sell, or vice versa) during the backtest period.
- **Significance:** A higher number of trades provides more statistical significance to the results, but also implies higher transaction costs (fees/slippage).

### 4. Net Profit %
The total return generated by the strategy over the entire period, expressed as a percentage of the initial capital.
- **Formula:** `((Final Capital - Initial Capital) / Initial Capital) * 100`
- **Significance:** The primary measure of the strategy's overall performance and growth.

### 5. Average % per Trade
The average profit or loss per trade, expressed as a percentage.
- **Formula:** `Sum of (Trade Return %) / Number of Trades`
- **Significance:** It indicates the expectancy of each individual trade. A positive average suggests a profitable strategy in the long run.
